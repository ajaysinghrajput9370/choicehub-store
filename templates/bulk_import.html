📦 Bulk Product Import

Upload Excel and ZIP to import multiple products at once.

1
Prepare Excel

Columns: SKU, Name, Selling Price, MRP, Stock, Category (SKU must match image filenames)

2
Prepare Images

Name images as SKU_1.jpg, SKU_2.jpg, etc. and zip them.

3
Upload & Import

Upload both files and click Import. System will match SKU and upload images automatically.

📌 How it works

  • System reads SKU from Excel.
  • Extracts ZIP and finds images starting with that SKU.
  • Uploads all matching images to Cloudinary and saves URLs in product.
  • If images missing, you'll see a warning.
